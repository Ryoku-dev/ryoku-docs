---
title: Dual-boot with Windows
description: Install Ryoku alongside Windows on one disk, in free space you made or by letting the installer shrink a partition. Windows keeps booting from the same menu.
icon: hard-drive
---

Ryoku installs alongside Windows on a single disk. Pick **Install alongside**
at the installer's **Disk strategy** step and Ryoku goes into free
(unallocated) space. If the disk has none, the installer can shrink one
partition for you to make room. Your other partitions are kept as they are,
and the Limine boot menu lists Ryoku and Windows.

<Warning>
Shrinking a partition changes your Windows disk. Back up anything you cannot
lose before you start, whichever way you make the room.
</Warning>

## What you need

- **UEFI and GPT.** Boot the Ryoku USB in UEFI mode, not BIOS/CSM/legacy. A
  UEFI Windows install already uses GPT.
- **Room for Ryoku:** at least a 20 GiB root plus a 2 GiB boot partition and
  your swap size. 64 GiB or more is comfortable. The whole disk must be at
  least 32 GiB.
- **Windows shut down cleanly.** Turn off Fast Startup in Windows (**Control
  Panel > Power Options > Choose what the power buttons do**, then untick
  **Turn on fast startup**) and use **Shut down**, not Restart or Sleep. A
  hibernated NTFS volume cannot be shrunk safely, and the installer refuses to
  touch it.
- **BitLocker:** suspend it in Windows first and keep your recovery key. With
  BitLocker on, booting Windows from the Ryoku menu asks for the recovery key.

No second disk is required. If you would rather keep the two systems fully
separate, put Ryoku on its own drive and choose **Erase whole disk** there.

## Step 1: Disable Secure Boot

Reboot into your firmware setup (usually `F2`, `Del` or `F12` at power-on),
turn **Secure Boot** off, save and exit. See [Install Ryoku](/docs/install) for
writing the USB.

## Step 2: Make room, either way

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Let the installer shrink a partition">
    Skip ahead to Step 3. When the disk has no free space, the **Disk layout**
    step lists the partitions it can shrink (NTFS, ext4 and btrfs). Select one
    and use `←` and `→` to set how much space Ryoku gets. The installer shrinks
    the filesystem first and the partition second, touches only that one
    partition, and stops at the first sign of trouble.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Shrink it yourself in Windows">
    In Windows press `Win + X`, open **Disk Management**, right-click your
    Windows (C:) volume and choose **Shrink Volume**. Shrink it by at least the
    space Ryoku needs (64 GiB is a comfortable amount). The **Unallocated**
    space that appears after the Windows partition is where Ryoku goes. Then
    shut Windows down fully.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Step 3: Install alongside Windows

Boot the Ryoku USB in UEFI mode. In the installer:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the target disk">
    At **Target disk**, choose the disk that holds Windows.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the alongside strategy">
    At **Disk strategy**, pick **Install alongside Windows** (or **Install
    alongside (keep existing OS)** on a disk with another system). Nothing is
    written until the final Review step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the layout, then press Tab">
    The **Disk layout** step shows the free space Ryoku will use, or the
    partition it will shrink and by how much. Adjust swap if you like, then
    press **Tab** to continue to Review. Review states exactly what will be
    created and kept; confirm there to install.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Ryoku creates its 2 GiB boot partition and root in the free region, installs
Limine with an entry for Windows, and leaves the Windows partitions as they
are (apart from the one you chose to shrink).

<Info>
A second, empty GPT drive with free space and no EFI partition of its own works
too: the installer offers **Install in the free space**, creates a new EFI
partition and root there, and leaves every existing partition untouched.
</Info>

## "I press Tab and nothing happens"

On the **Disk layout** step, Tab only continues once the layout is
installable. When it is blocked, the screen shows a red `⚠` line with the
reason. The usual causes:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Not enough free space and no partition selected">
    Select a partition in the list and use `←`/`→` to give Ryoku room, or make
    room in Windows first (Step 2).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="No partition can be shrunk safely">
    The reasons are listed under each partition. The usual one is a Windows
    volume that is hibernated or marked dirty: turn off Fast Startup, run a
    full shutdown, and try again. BitLocker-locked volumes are not shrunk.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The disk is not GPT, or you booted in BIOS mode">
    Alongside installs need UEFI and GPT. Boot the USB in UEFI mode, or press
    `esc` and choose **Erase whole disk** on a dedicated drive.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The disk is smaller than 32 GiB">
    Ryoku needs at least 32 GiB. Pick a larger disk.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## After install

The Limine boot menu appears at power-on with entries for **Ryoku** and
**Windows**; pick whichever you want. If Windows does not appear, boot Ryoku and
run `ryoku doctor`, then see [Troubleshoot](/docs/troubleshoot).
