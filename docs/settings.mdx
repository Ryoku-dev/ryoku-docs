Press `Mod + ,` to open **Ryoku Settings**, the Hub: one window for the whole
desktop. Most pages apply as you change them; the window manager page applies
when you press **Save**.

The Hub writes your choices through the window manager's own config
(`settings.lua` on Hyprland, `settings.kdl` on niri) and the shell's settings.
Your own `user.lua` or `user.kdl` still loads last and wins, so hand edits there
override what the Hub writes. See [Customize](/docs/customize) for that
layering.

<Tip>
  The search box at the top of the rail finds any page or option, so typing
  "blur" or "night light" jumps straight to it. The **Advanced** switch at the
  foot of the rail shows the deeper pages and options.
</Tip>

## One Hub per window manager

The Hub only offers what the running window manager can do. Its compositor page
is named after it (Hyprland, Niri or Wayfire) and holds every setting that
window manager owns; options it cannot honour are hidden or disabled rather
than left dead. Keybinds sit outside that group: the same shortcuts work on
every window manager.

## The pages

### Overview

| Page | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| Profile | Your machine at a glance (host, CPU, GPU, memory, uptime) on a plate you can edit and share. |
| General | Language, regional formats, location, fonts, icon theme and desktop sounds. |
| Updates | Pending Ryoku updates, waiting distribution packages, your channel and the run log. |

### Devices

| Page | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| Displays | Resolution, refresh rate, scale, rotation, arrangement and mirroring, plus night light. |
| Connections | Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, your hotspot and your phone. |
| Input | Keyboard layout, mouse and touchpad feel, key repeat and gestures. |
| Graphics & Power | Which GPU renders the desktop, hybrid and passthrough modes, power profiles and the battery charge limit. |

Displays, Input and Graphics & Power are covered in depth on
[Displays & hardware](/docs/hardware).

### Look

| Page | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| Animations | Motion and bezier curves, with a live preview. |
| Lockscreen | The skin your lock screen wears. It changes the look, never your login. |

Wallpapers, color schemes and rices live in the wallpaper picker (`Mod + W`);
see [Themes & wallpapers](/docs/theming).

### Compositor

| Page | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| Window manager | How windows look and behave: gaps, borders, rounding, opacity, blur, shadows and layout. Its **Compositor** tab switches window manager. |
| Plugins | Hyprland plugins such as title bars, glass, focus flash and key sounds. Hyprland only. |
| Layer Rules | Bars, launchers and other layer surfaces by namespace: blur, shadow and opacity. Advanced. |

### Desktop

| Page | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| Bar Studio | The bar style, its rails, zones and widgets, and the dock. |
| Desktop | The brand mark, the reload cover, notifications and the clipboard. |
| Controls | The quick settings panel on `Mod + Escape`: its sections and their order. |
| App Launcher | The launcher's home view, greeting and weather. |

Desktop widgets, the depth effect and the visualizer are arranged on the
desktop itself: right-click the wallpaper and choose **Edit desktop**.

### Keys & apps

| Page | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| Keybinds | Every shortcut, read live, your own binds, and the apps behind the browser, terminal, editor and files keys. |
| App Overrides | Per-app opacity, blur and corners. Advanced. |
| Window Rules | Float, pin, size or place windows by class or title. Advanced. |

### System

| Page | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| Performance | Trade some eye candy for lower CPU, GPU and memory use, or follow the power profile. |
| Session | Commands that run at login and session environment variables. |
| Recording | Screen recording quality, frame rate and codec. |
| Dictation | Voice typing. |
| Fastfetch | The terminal system readout and its logo. Advanced. |
| Import config | Bring in your existing dotfiles (kitty, fish, Fastfetch and more), with undo. Advanced. |

### Extend

| Page | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| Add-ons | Your installed plugins and bundles: turn them on, place them, update or remove them. **Browse RyoStore** opens the store for more. |
| Rashin | Turn Rashin on or off and set up the Hermes agent. |

[Rashin](/docs/rashin) has its own walkthrough, and keeping the system current
is covered on [Updates](/docs/updates). **Credits** sits at the foot of the
rail.
