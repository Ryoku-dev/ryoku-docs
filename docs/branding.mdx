## Name

**Ryoku** comes from the Japanese kanji **力** (read *chikara* or *ryoku*),
meaning "power" or "strength". The tagline appears in both Japanese and
English:

> **力と美のために** : *For the sake of power and beauty.*

Use "Ryoku" in prose. Name an edition by what it runs on: "Ryoku on Arch
Linux", "Ryoku on CachyOS", "Ryoku on Void", "Ryoku on Fedora". The old "Ryoku
Arch" name is retired now that Ryoku runs on four distributions. Never put
another project's name inside Ryoku's product name (no "Ryoku Fedora"), and
never imply that another project endorses Ryoku.

The expected tone is polished, direct and usable for daily machine work. No
marketing fluff, no emoji.

## The marks

| Mark | What it is | Where it lives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The 力 seal** | The kanji drawn as a stencil cut in bone, with one cut in burnt orange. | `ryoku/assets/brand/logo-mark-v2.png` |
| **The wordmark** | `RYOKU` in the same stencil letterforms, the K's leg in burnt orange. | `ryoku/assets/brand/wordmark-v2.png` |
| **The Rashin seal** | A sibling seal for Rashin: the same tile carrying 十, the needle and compass card, with north in vermillion. | `ryoku/assets/brand/rashin-mark.svg`, `rashin-lockup.svg` |

The seal is the primary mark. The wordmark is for places that already have room
and typographic context: a site header, a banner, these docs. When in doubt,
use the seal.

The docs use the wordmark in the header, recolored to near-black ink for the
light theme, and the seal on a near-black rounded square as the favicon.

## Colors

A few colors never follow the wallpaper:

| Token | Value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vermillion (`Theme.brand`) | `#e2342a` | The color the 力 seal wears in the shell. |
| Alert (`Tokens.alert`) | `#e2342a` | Badges and warnings, so an alert always reads as one. |
| Kintsugi gold | `#d9a441` | Seams and highlights, used sparingly. |

Everything else is derived from the wallpaper by matugen, so the desktop
recolors while the brand stays put. A sun is a sun on any wallpaper. Before a
palette exists, the compiled defaults are black paper (`#000000`) and bone ink
(`#cdc4ba`); see [Themes & wallpapers](/docs/theming).

## Typography and cursor

- **Display headlines:** Fraunces.
- **Interface:** Space Grotesk.
- **Code and labels:** JetBrains Mono Nerd Font.
- **Fallback and CJK:** Noto, which renders the 力 consistently on every
  install.
- **Cursor:** Bibata.

These are fixed. The palette changes with the wallpaper; the marks, the
typefaces and the cursor do not.

## What "on-brand" means

1. **The accent the eye lands on first is the vermillion.** It sits on the
   frame and the masthead, never spread across the content.
2. **Depth is a hairline, not a shadow.** Surfaces are flat with a thin bone
   line; a shadow appears only where something genuinely floats.
3. **The 力 is the primary mark.** Use it as a mark, not as decoration.
4. **No emoji as a logo.** The seal is the mark.

## Don't-do list

- Don't recolor the seal or the wordmark beyond the light and dark variants.
- Don't substitute a different kanji or character. 力 is the mark.
- Don't add gradients, outlines or shadows to the marks.
- Don't introduce another fixed accent. Everything that is not the brand
  follows the wallpaper.
- Don't use the vermillion for decoration; it means brand or alert.
