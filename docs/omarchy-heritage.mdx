Ryoku's runtime, command surface, installer and shell are Ryoku-owned. Selected
ideas and code are adapted from upstream projects, each credited in the
repository's [`NOTICE`](https://github.com/Ryoku-dev/ryoku/blob/main/NOTICE),
which is the authoritative list.

## Where Ryoku comes from

Ryoku's alpha series (through v0.1.1-alpha) began as a fork of
[Omarchy](https://github.com/basecamp/omarchy) by David Heinemeier Hansson and
contributors. The beta tree was pruned and rebuilt from an empty root: its
installer, shell, theming, boot art, tooling and system definition are Ryoku's
own.

## How to read names and surfaces

- Current runtime behavior is Ryoku-owned. Anything you use day to day belongs
  to the Ryoku surface: the `ryoku` command, Ryoku Settings, the shell, Ryogami,
  Ryostore and the signed Ryoku repositories.
- Historical names that still appear in the tree are there for attribution,
  legal hygiene, or because they point at external objects that still exist.
- The `LICENSE` and `NOTICE` files at the repo root are required attribution.
  They stay.

## What does not ship as active runtime

These names belong to the lineage, not to the current desktop:

- **Waybar** is not the bar. The bar is one of the shell's bar styles.
- **Mako** is not the notifier. Notifications surface through the shell.
- **SwayOSD** is not used.
- **Tofi, Walker and Elephant** are not the launcher. The launcher is part of
  the shell, on `Mod + Space`.
- Omarchy package repositories, keyrings and mirrors are not part of Ryoku's
  update plumbing. Ryoku ships from its own signed repositories.

If you find documentation, scripts or screenshots that reference these names as
live runtime, treat them as stale.

## How to review a historical reference

When you find a legacy name in the tree, classify it before changing it:

1. **Attribution.** Keep it if it preserves required notices or upstream credit.
2. **External identifier.** Keep package, repo or remote IDs that must stay
   aligned with published upstream objects.
3. **Active runtime.** Anything that affects current behavior belongs in a
   Ryoku-owned surface and should not leak historical naming. Rename or remove
   it.
