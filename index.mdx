**力と美のために** : *for the sake of power and beauty.*

Ryoku is a hand-built Linux desktop. One shell runs on
[Hyprland](https://hypr.land/) or [niri](https://github.com/YaLTeR/niri)
(Wayfire is experimental), on four distributions, and all of it comes from one
repository: the desktop, the installers and the system definition.

The desktop is one continuous surface. The bar, the panels, the launcher, the
lock screen and the session controls move together and retint from your
wallpaper. Every setting lives in Ryoku Settings, and the `ryoku` command keeps
the machine updated, snapshotted and healthy.

## Editions

| Edition | Compositors | Get it | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Arch Linux | Hyprland, niri, Wayfire | signed ISO, or the one-line installer | stable |
| CachyOS | Hyprland, niri, Wayfire | signed ISO, or the one-line installer | stable |
| Void Linux (glibc, runit) | niri | signed ISO, or the one-line installer | experimental |
| Fedora® 44 and 45 | Hyprland, niri | the one-line installer | experimental |

Every edition is `x86_64` and installs the same desktop from a signed native
repository (pacman, XBPS or RPM). A community port for NixOS lives at
[Ryoku on NixOS](https://aethctl.github.io/Ryoku-on-NixOS/).

## The keys that open almost everything

| Keys | Opens |
|---|---|
| `Super + Space` | The launcher: apps, commands, files, packages and the calculator |
| `Super + ,` | Ryoku Settings |
| `Super + Esc` | Quick settings: session, network, sound and power |
| `Super + W` | The Ryogami wallpaper picker |
| `Super + K` | Kit: keybinds, commands, your phone and dev tools |
| `Alt + Space` | Ask Rashin |
